# Design Philosophy

The website must **not** feel like a traditional musician portfolio.

It must feel like the visitor has intercepted a forbidden transmission from Neo‑Astra City in the year 2097.

The experience should gradually reveal the world while constantly encouraging visitors to listen to Faviax.

The music is always the center of the experience.

---

# Project Overview

Create a premium, immersive, single-page website for **Faviax**, a cinematic synthwave music project built around the science-fiction universe of **Neo-Astra City**.

The website has two equally important objectives:

1. Explain the complete Faviax universe.
2. Convert visitors into listeners across every official streaming platform.

The result must feel like a classified archive recovered from the future.

---

# Core Narrative Concept

## Canonical Setting

Neo‑Astra City (2097) is a vertical megacity where emotions are measured, regulated and weaponized.

Important concepts:

- E‑Lattice
- AURA
- Project EIDOLON
- Eclipse Array

Music is capable of modifying reality by interfering with emotional frequencies.

## Main Characters

- Aeron Vale
